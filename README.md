## 1. Executive Overview & Mission
The **ORIC Industrial Intelligence & Opportunity Assessment Platform (ORIC-IOAP)** is a production-oriented, full-stack enterprise web application built for the **Office of Research, Innovation & Commercialization (ORIC)** at **Salim Habib University (SHU)** in Karachi, Pakistan.

The platform bridges academic research generated by faculty members, researchers, and students across the **Faculty of Pharmacy (FoP)** and sister departments with Karachi’s robust industrial corridors and Pakistan's commercial healthcare and industrial markets.

### The 5 Core Questions Answered by the Platform:
1. **What valuable technologies and innovations are contained in our university's research?**
2. **Which tangible commercial products and industrial testing services can be developed from them?**
3. **Which industries and industrial areas in Karachi (Korangi, Landhi, S.I.T.E., Federal B, North Karachi, Port Qasim, KEPZ, Bin Qasim, Dhabeji SEZ) are the primary prospective partners and customers?**
4. **What are the credible bottom-up market opportunities (TAM/SAM/SOM) and 5-year growth scenarios (2026–2031)?**
5. **What actionable commercialization pathways (licensing, incubation, patent filing, contract R&D) should ORIC, faculty, entrepreneurs, and industrial partners pursue next?**

## 3. Technology Stack & Design Architecture

### Frontend
- **Structure:** Semantic HTML5 with accessible visual hierarchy and proper `<h1-h6>` heading nesting.
- **Styling:** Custom Vanilla CSS3 Design System with a **Light Professional University-Enterprise Theme**:
  - *SHU Navy:* `#0A2540`
  - *Academic Blue:* `#0284C7`
  - *Innovation Teal / Emerald:* `#0D9488` / `#10B981`
  - *Clean Light Slate Background:* `#F8FAFC`
  - *Card Surfaces:* `#FFFFFF` with crisp `#E2E8F0` borders
- **Typography:** Modern Google Fonts (`Inter` for body copy, `Outfit` for institutional headings).
- **Data Visualizations:** Chart.js 4.4.2 responsive charts for:
  - Therapeutic domain and formulation distribution (Doughnut chart)
  - Karachi industrial area opportunities (Horizontal bar chart)
  - Interactive 5-year CAGR scenario projections (Dynamic line graph)
- **Zero-Latency Dual Mode:** Runs either through the Express REST API or standalone directly in any browser with preloaded data (`data/data_bundle.js`).

### Backend (REST API)
- **Runtime:** Node.js v20+ with Express 5
- **File Upload Engine:** `multer` supporting PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, TXT, and CSV (with malware scanning and format validation)
- **AI Document Intelligence Engine:** Configurable layer supporting **Google Gemini API** (`gemini-2.5-flash` / `gemini-2.5-pro`) alongside an embedded pharmaceutical domain rules engine.
- **REST Endpoints:**
  - `GET /api/health` — System status and database size
  - `GET /api/stats` — Executive dashboard statistics
  - `GET /api/projects` — Filterable project list (`?q=`, `?course=`, `?domain=`, `?priority=`, `?area=`)
  - `GET /api/projects/:id` — Detailed project profile
  - `PUT /api/projects/:id` — Update commercial scores, review notes, and status
  - `POST /api/upload` — Ingest new document dossiers via multi-stage pipeline
  - `POST /api/analyze` — Trigger live AI extraction and assessment
  - `GET /api/industrial-areas` — 10 verified Karachi industrial zones
  - `GET /api/sectors` — 22 Pakistani industrial sectors
  - `GET /api/evidence` — Traceable citation sources

## 4. Multi-Page Navigation & Core Modules

1. **Executive Dashboard (`dashboard`)**
   - 5 KPI summary cards (Projects, Addressable Market, Products, High-Priority Deals, Industrial Estates).
   - Dynamic Chart.js visualizations with live currency conversion (PKR / USD).
   - High-Priority Innovation Spotlight cards with one-click AI dossier inspection.

2. **Upload & Processing Pipeline (`upload`)**
   - Multi-file drag-and-drop zone.
   - 6-Stage Automated Processing Pipeline:
     1. Format & Malware Scan
     2. Text & Structural Extraction
     3. Scanned PDF & OCR Detection
     4. AI Concept Extraction
     5. Karachi Cluster Fit
     6. Commercial Scoring
   - Metadata intake form (PI, Department, Domain, TRL level 1–9, IP Status, Funding).
   - "Re-Sync Excel Data" button to instantly reload the FoP LMS Fall 2026 dataset.

3. **Document Library & Repository (`library`)**
   - Full search grid with multi-faceted filtering:
     - Course selection (19 courses)
     - Research domain selection
     - Priority level (High, Medium, Standard)
     - Karachi industrial area
   - Client-side pagination and CSV export capability.

4. **AI Intelligence Engine & Deep Dossier (`intelligence`)**
   - Section A: Executive Summary & Clinical Unmet Need
   - Section B: Technology & Scientific Principles, Novelty, and TRL Validation
   - Section C: Commercial Products (Unit manufacturing cost, selling price range, competitors, differentiation, margin %)
   - Section D: Industrial Testing Services (ICH Zone IVb stability testing, HPLC assays)
   - Section E: Karachi Industrial Fit & Candidate Corporate Partners
   - Section F: Validation & Testing Checklist
   - Export structured dossier as JSON.

5. **Products & Services Commercial Catalog (`products`)**
   - Marketplace catalog of spun-off products categorized by dosage form (Effervescent tablets, Oral Dissolving Films, Chewable Gummies, Wound Biopolymers, Cosmeceuticals, Digital Health AI).

6. **Industry Applications & 22 Sectors Taxonomy (`industries`)**
   - Comprehensive cross-mapping to 22 Pakistani industrial sectors with national market sizes and CAGRs.
   - Interactive Industry-by-Product matrix table detailing local manufacturing feasibility and import-substitution foreign exchange savings.

7. **Karachi Industrial Area Intelligence (`karachi`)**
   - 10 verified industrial clusters:
     1. *Korangi Industrial Area (KATI)*
     2. *Landhi Industrial Area (LATI)*
     3. *S.I.T.E. Industrial Area (SAI)*
     4. *Federal B Area Industrial Zone (FBATI)*
     5. *North Karachi Industrial Area (NKATI)*
     6. *Port Qasim Industrial & Port Zone (PQA)*
     7. *Karachi Export Processing Zone (KEPZ)*
     8. *Bin Qasim Industrial Park (NIP)*
     9. *Dhabeji Special Economic Zone (CPEC SEZ)*
     10. *Super Highway Industrial Corridor*
   - Detailed diagnostics: coordinates, associations, verified sectors, utilities, logistics constraints, technology adoption potential, and matched SHU research projects.

8. **Five-Year Growth Forecasting & Market Sizing (`forecasting`)**
   - Bottom-up TAM, SAM, and SOM calculation deck.
   - Mathematical formula: $\text{Market Size}_n = \text{Base} \times (1 + r)^n$.
   - Interactive CAGR % slider that recalculates the 2026–2031 forecast table and dynamic Chart.js chart in real time.
   - Currency toggle: Pakistani Rupee (PKR) and US Dollars ($ USD at 1 USD = 280 PKR).

9. **Commercialization Assessment & Scoring Framework (`commercialization`)**
   - 8-Criteria evaluation scoring matrix with interactive sliders:
     - Technical Feasibility (15%)
     - Market Attractiveness (20%)
     - Customer Demand Evidence (15%)
     - TRL Maturity (10%)
     - Intellectual Property (15%)
     - Team & Facilities (5%)
     - Industrial Scalability (10%)
     - Import Substitution (10%)
   - Real-time weighted composite score gauge and strategic pathway classification.
   - Official ORIC committee review notes and decision logging.

10. **Professional PDF Reports & Downloads (`reports`)**
    - Generators for Reports A through H:
      - *Report A: Executive Opportunity Brief*
      - *Report B: Detailed Commercialization Report*
      - *Report C: Industry-Specific Report*
      - *Report D: Karachi Industrial-Area Report*
      - *Report E: Product & Service Market Assessment*
      - *Report F: Five-Year Growth Forecast*
      - *Report G: Investor & Industry Partnership Deck*
      - *Report H: Multi-Project Portfolio Ranking*
    - Printable preview sheet formatted with Salim Habib University crest, official reference numbers, signature blocks, and disclaimers.

11. **Data Sources, Evidence & Assumptions Registry (`sources`)**
    - Sourced market evidence from PBS, SBP, DRAP, PPMA, TDAP, KATI, and PSX company disclosures.
    - Documented macroeconomic assumptions log.

## 5. Quick Startup & Deployment Instructions

### Option A: Node.js Express Server (Recommended)
1. Open a terminal in the project directory:
   ```powershell
   cd "d:\sir azhar project"
   ```
2. Start the production server:
   ```powershell
   npm start
   ```
3. Open your browser and navigate to:
   ```
   http://localhost:3000
   ```

### Option B: Standalone Browser Launch (Zero-Configuration)
- Simply double-click `index.html` or open `d:/sir azhar project/index.html` in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox.
- The preloaded institutional data bundle (`data/data_bundle.js`) will immediately initialize the full database with all 233 projects, 10 industrial zones, and interactive charts without requiring an internet connection or backend server!
